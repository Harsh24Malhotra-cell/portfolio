[01] — THE THEOREM

Two states. Any tree.
A new upper bound.

Any N-ary tree can be traversed by a two-state Turing machine using at most N + 1 tape symbols.
First proved with a bound of 2N symbols, the first known upper bound for this class of generalised Turing machines. Later tightened to N + 1 and submitted to MIT's Undergraduate Research Technology Conference.
Built during the Wolfram High School Research Program (Boston, Jun–Jul 2026), supervised by Lyman Hurd, former MIT professor and Google software engineer.
